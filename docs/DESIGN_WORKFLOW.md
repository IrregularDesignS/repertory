# How to use the Repertory staff library

A Repertory project begins with its subject rather than with the Gnomon. What deserves to be seen, who made it, and why does it matter? Those questions determine how much of the institution should be visible. An artist's photographic language and a venue's independent identity are not secondary to a branch color.

Once the curatorial relationship is clear, identify the relevant branch. Concepts, Pressing, Provisions and Social are organizational designations with fixed palette names. The Color Lab is useful when a composition needs to be developed or checked against Light, Midtone, and Dark environments and the four authored variations. Copy the specifications only after the actual treatment has been selected and inspected.

When an application requires a Gnomon-based frame or geometric abstraction, the original SVGs remain the highest authority. The Gnomon Builder explores controlled endpoint extensions with fixed stem thickness, consistent gaps and modular gridded/lined forms. The square takes the available space while retaining the geometric logic of the original. An approximation that fills every last pixel is less valuable than one that preserves the module.

In an editorial application, maintain proportionality. The subject should command the image, and Repertory should provide structure, attribution, and legible information. Paragraph-scale type needs contrast appropriate to its field; carefully composed negative space is preferable to extra decorative rules. Keep cropping responsive and protective of important features, particularly faces and gestures.

A designer's final handoff should name the source vector, branch, theme, composition, exported assets, and any assumptions. A prototype is not automatically an approved master. A speculative collaboration is not a genuine announcement. Communicate those distinctions in the finished work.
