# Source, authorship, and distribution notes

## What governs the identity

The project does not give creative latitude to invent new Repertory geometry. The original Gnomon, wordmark and four subsidiary SVGs are the master drawings. `repertory-prompt-system.pdf` supplies formal rules; `repertory-toolkit.pdf` demonstrates construction, grid, patterns and speculative applications; the later documented corrections in the handoff and accepted tools resolve the current implementations.

The original filled combination and original stroked combination are available in `assets/source-vectors/`. The twelve figure/ground assets per mode in `assets/marks/` preserve the original solid Gnomon and wordmark **paths** while substituting the system's exact approved colors. These assets are presentation conveniences, not a new geometric master or an independent logo redesign.

The four branches and their color names are Concepts/Patina, Pressing/Marquee, Provisions/Apertivo and Social/Gala. Vellum, India, Silver, and Carbon are shared neutrals. The four canonical compositions are Complex, Layered, Restrained, Minimal. The original authored SVGs are in `assets/stickers/`, organized into Light, Midtone, and Dark sets.

## What is an illustration rather than a claim

The Midori/Gyuune Cassette, Matty Matheson and Bottom of the Hill passages are **internal creative scenarios and design references**. They do not indicate a real Repertory partnership, acquisition, live event, commission, concert restoration, or authorization by the pictured people and organizations. Treat their photographs and venue identities as belonging to their respective creators and rights holders.

The website's new editorial studies are proposed implementations, not new approved brand exemplars. They are intentionally designed to keep each subject's own identity intact while demonstrating the restrained scale of institutional attribution.

## Rights-sensitive source files

Photographs originate in the user's provided asset kit. These are incorporated as optimized WebP copies for internal design-review use; the package does not grant public redistribution rights to these images. The same caution applies to the included PDFs and any included third-party logos, names or art. Check rights before creating a publicly accessible Pages site.

The user also supplied a typeface collection. **No font files are in this package.** The declared font hierarchy remains Source Sans Pro for Repertory display/brand voice and Meta Pro for reading/body text according to the brand guide. The site uses neutral system-font fallbacks until properly licensed webfont files are available.

## Internal site security

This is a static library, not a login system. HTML `noindex` metadata and `robots.txt` are not security. Do not treat GitHub Pages deployment as employee-only access without separately confirming an appropriate access-control solution.
